How I work

No big strategies to start. Just clarity, then structure.

Most owners I meet aren't short of work. They're short of a clear view of the whole business. That's where we start.

The process

How we'll work together

  1. A 30-minute call

    We talk about what's going on in your business, where you want it to be, and whether I'm the right person to help. No obligation.

  2. Alignment

    We get everything out of your head and agree what matters most: where you are now, where you want to be and what's in the way. Your time, your finances and your team, in one clear picture.

  3. Your coaching programme

    One-to-one or group coaching, whichever suits you, with clear actions to take away each time and someone holding you to them.

What we work on

Time, finances, team and marketing

Every business problem I see comes back to one of these four. We look at them together, because they're connected. Tap each one to see more.

1Time

Where your week actually goes, what genuinely needs you, and what can come off your plate.

  • Where it goes
  • What matters
  • What drains you
2Finance

Knowing your numbers, so the bank balance stops being the only thing you judge the business on.

  • What's coming in
  • What's going out
  • What's left as profit
3Team

The right people in the right roles, so the business runs without you standing over it.

  • Clear roles

4Marketing

  • Getting found
  • The right enquiries
