There was a time I would never have got on a stage

You know your trade inside out. Running the business is a different skill, and nobody ever taught you it.

There was a time in my life I would never have got on a stage……..No chance!!!

I'm a high yellow …. so you'd think it came naturally…It didn't. It wasn't that I didn't know what to talk about - I just had to learn the skill of standing in front of people and saying it…. Badly at first 😀

It's exactly the same for the business owners I work with…..You know your trade inside out……Nobody questions that…Best sparky, best builder, best roofer in the area

BUT running the business is a different skill…..And nobody ever taught you it or more to the point you never took the time to really learn it !

So it's the same week on repeat….Same 60 hours…same cash flow panic…same Sunday night dread…..Groundhog Day….

It doesn't have to be like this …. You just need to learn the business bit !!

There's an assessment on my website that shows you what type of boss you are …. your percentages, your strengths and your blind spots…..Why you avoid the planning…..Why some jobs drain you and others don't…..No sign-up…no contact details needed to get result (as I hate it when you give contact details - fear of spam emails !!) ….. Take the assessment here.

It doesn't have to stay like this…..The changes aren't as hard as you think…..You just need to know where to start.
