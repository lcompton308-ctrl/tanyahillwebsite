Questions

Before you book

How does the first call work?

It's a 30-minute conversation with no obligation. Message me on WhatsApp and we'll find a time that suits you.

Who do you work with?

Owners of established trade and construction businesses who have plenty of work but not enough profit, time or headspace. Many of my clients turn over seven figures.

I'm not a numbers person. Is that a problem?

Not at all. We start by getting everything out of your head and onto paper, often as an illustration, so you can see what's going on without wading through spreadsheets.

What is Which Boss Are You?

A 12-question assessment based on Thomas Erikson's Surrounded by Idiots. It shows your natural behaviour colour and how you work, lead and communicate. Take it here.
