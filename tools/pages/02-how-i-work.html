How I work

No big strategies to start. Just clarity, then structure.

Most owners I meet aren't short of work. They're short of a clear view of the whole business. That's where we start.

The process

How we'll work together

  1. First call

    A 30-minute call. We talk about what's going on in your business, where you want it to be, and whether I'm the right person to help. No obligation.

  2. Diagnosing and alignment

    We look properly at your time, finances, team and marketing to find what's really holding the business back. We get everything out of your head and agree what matters most: where you are now, where you want to be and what's in the way, in one clear picture.

  3. Coaching

    One-to-one or group coaching, whichever suits you, with clear actions to take away each time and someone holding you to them.
