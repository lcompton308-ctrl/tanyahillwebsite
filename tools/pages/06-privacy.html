Privacy policy

Your information, kept simple

This website doesn't use cookies or tracking. It only collects information when you choose to contact me or send me your Colour Profile results.

Last updated 30 October 2026

Who I am

The company is the "controller" of any personal information you share through this website, which means I'm responsible for it. You can contact me about privacy at {{EMAIL}} or on {{PHONE}}.

What this website collects

  • No cookies, analytics or tracking. I don't use Google Analytics, advertising pixels or anything similar.
  • Your own device. If you take the Colour Profile, your browser's local storage remembers your name and your latest results link so you don't have to retype them. This stays on your device and isn't sent to me. You can clear it with the "Forget my details on this device" button on the Colour Profile page, or through your browser settings.
  • The booking helper only writes a WhatsApp message on your own device. Nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp.
  • The Colour Profile keeps your name and results on your own device, and only reaches me if you choose to send it. See the next section.
  • Fonts and images are hosted on this website, so no third-party font service sees your visit.

When you take the Colour Profile

At the end of the quiz I ask for your name, so your results page is personal to you. Your name and answers stay on your own device: nothing is sent to me automatically.

If you choose to send me your results (the "Send my results to Tanya" button opens WhatsApp with them written in), I receive your name, your colour scores, your results link and your WhatsApp number. I use them to talk you through your profile, which you've asked for by sending them. My lawful basis is my legitimate interest in responding to that request. I won't add you to a mailing list or use your details for marketing unless you ask me to.

Your colour is worked out automatically from your answers. It's a conversation starter, not an assessment, and no decision about you is made from it.

If we don't go on to work together, I delete your Colour Profile results within 12 months. If we do, they become part of your client record (see below). I'll delete them sooner if you ask.

Your results link. Your results page link includes your name and colour scores. Anyone you share the link with can see them.

When you contact me

If you message me on WhatsApp, call, email or message me on LinkedIn, I'll receive the details you choose to share, such as your name, phone number, email address and information about your business. If you send me your Colour Profile result, I'll receive that too.

I use this information to reply to you, arrange calls and, if we work together, to deliver coaching. My lawful basis is my legitimate interest in responding to your enquiry, taking steps at your request before we agree to work together, and then performing our coaching agreement.

I keep enquiry details only for as long as needed to deal with them. Client records are kept for the length of our work together and afterwards for as long as the law requires, for example for tax and accounting.

Who else is involved

  • WhatsApp (WhatsApp Ireland Ltd, part of Meta) carries messages you send through it. Its privacy policy applies to your use of WhatsApp.
  • My email provider stores the emails you send me on my behalf.
  • GitHub (GitHub, Inc., USA) hosts this website. Like any web host, it may log technical details such as your IP address for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok and Facebook apply their own privacy policies if you follow the links to my profiles.
  • ActionCOACH UK. I'm a certified ActionCOACH business coach. If you join a programme delivered through ActionCOACH, the details needed to deliver it may be shared with them.

I never sell your information.

Information sent outside the UK

Some of these services are based outside the UK, so your information may be transferred abroad. GitHub, Inc. in the USA protects transfers under the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses. WhatsApp Ireland is in the EU, which the UK recognises as having adequate protection.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see the information I hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, object to or limit how I use it, or receive a copy to take elsewhere. Just get in touch using the details above. It's free, and I'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how I've handled your information, please tell me first so I can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes
