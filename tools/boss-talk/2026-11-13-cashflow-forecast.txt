Nearly every business owner says this to me … and I hate to burst their "I'm different" bubble but …. Well …. They aren't different !

Building a CASHFLOW FORECAST is a must …. Judging how well your business is doing from your bank balance is a def No No !

I do honestly understand that most businesses can't predict what's going to come in over the next 3 months BUT that's why I'm better at this than you 😊 this is my job …. Don't ask me to build a house, fit a bathroom, rewire a house (or even change a plug!), landscape a garden or quite frankly do anything that is at all practical BUT if you want more TURNOVER, more PROFIT, better CASHFLOW, to attract more grade A work, create an amazing TEAM or your TIME back ….. then without a doubt I am the girl for the job !!

I really don't know why tradie business owners spend years learning their trade but expect to just automatically know how to run a successful profitable business …… I mean I say this from experience because that's what I did with my tradie business for 12 years until I learnt how to actually run the structure of my business 😊

If you want things to be different then you have to learn some different stuff !
