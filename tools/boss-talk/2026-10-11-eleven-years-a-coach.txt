Today I have officially been a certified Business coach for 11 years, after being a client 3 years before that ! 10 years ago I decided to niche into the construction and tradie world as that was my background and I've never looked back ! What a rollercoaster with my first year feeling like I was paddling up river without a paddle … but in business you just keep going, learning every step of the way and I can honestly say I love what I do … yes I earn good money because I'm good at what I do but that is only a byproduct ….my real driver and biggest buzz is going on a journey with my clients and seeing them get everything they want … just like a coach did for me back in the day 🥰

I'm still planning just like I did 14 years ago when I entered this world and I'm still doing it the old school way now with pen and paper … unusual in a high tech world …. New 1 yr plan nailed !!!

LOVE WHAT I DO !!
